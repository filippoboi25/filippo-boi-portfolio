Interactive visual systems

Nebula

Site-specific installation — Spoleto, 2022

Role Collective concept · Interactive visual system development
With Elisa Cau, Paulina Herrera, Michele Mandrelli, Davide Porta, Valerie Tameu
Year 2022
01 — The idea

Bodies, space and machines communicating through data and movement.

Nebula was born from an artistic residency and develops over time until the staging about a year later. It is an installation system in which performer dancers interact with the environment through wearable sensors, activating a generative visual system that reacts and transforms in real time.

A sensitive ecosystem, in which gesture, code and visual matter coincide — where the human and the machine don't oppose each other but coexist.

02 — The system

"Human and machine coexisting — not in opposition, but in dialogue. The sensor as a common language between the dancing body and the responding space."

In a crypt set up for the event, two dancers open a dialogue based on the work's concept. Wearable sensors transmit movement data to a sculpture by Paulina Herrera, video-mapped with a solid that reacts and transforms following the performer's gesture.

The scenario shifts to a second environment where interaction happens with a robotic arm — a mechanical jellyfish conceived by Davide Porta. The performance closes by handing the sensors to the audience: anyone can become part of the system.

03 — Gallery
04 — The experience

The crypt changes its nature when the system is active. The dancers' movement is not limited to the physical space — it propagates into the sculpture, the light, the sound.

The audience observes a dialogue between organic bodies and artificial structures that responds in real time to every variation in gesture.

At the end of the performance the sensors are handed to the audience. The boundary between spectator and performer dissolves — anyone can activate the system, modify its behaviour, become part of the ecosystem.

Not a conclusion, but an opening.
