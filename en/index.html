Approach

01 Systemic

Every project is a system before a single output. I build coherent visual languages, not isolated solutions.

02 Relational

Design is born from contact with people. I listen, observe and translate into form what emerges from the relationship.

03 Experimental

Research precedes and informs execution. The process is not a means — it is part of the result.

04 Narrative

Every project tells something before it communicates. Meaning comes before form.

Profile

I design visual systems to give form, coherence and direction to communication. My interest in the visual spans graphic design, painting, environmental installations and photography. This contamination influences my approach to design — both conceptual and formal. Every project is a visual system before it is a single output.

Based in Bologna.
